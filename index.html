Ngebahas Gadget Asik · @ngegas.tech

Agent Ngegas is the internal publishing tool of the ngegas.tech channel. Our team writes and produces short gadget explainer videos in Indonesian. After a human editor approves a finished video, Agent Ngegas posts it with its caption to the channel's own TikTok account.

The tool is used only by the ngegas.tech team, on accounts the team owns. It is not offered to other TikTok users.
